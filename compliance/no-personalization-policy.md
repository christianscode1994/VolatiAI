# No-Personalization Policy — MiCA Boundary

**Policy date:** 2026-10-27
**Owner:** Solo developer/operator, VolatiAI
**Regulatory context:** MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114), FIN-FSA guidance
**Jurisdiction:** Finland

## Purpose

VolatiAI's legal position depends on remaining outside the scope of MiCA's
licensing requirements. The licensing trigger is the provision of
**personalized** crypto-asset advice. This policy formalizes the boundary
and commits the operator to staying on the non-personalized side of it.

## The line under MiCA

General one-to-many information published to an unknown audience is not
crypto-asset advice requiring authorization. The licensing requirement is
triggered when a service provides recommendations tailored to an individual
user's financial situation, knowledge, or objectives.

## VolatiAI's operational commitments

The operator commits to the following, without exception:

1. **Broadcast-only architecture.** All output is generated once and
   published identically to all followers. No user-specific output is
   produced.

2. **No personalized recommendations.** The system will not generate,
   suggest, or imply a recommendation for any individual user, including
   by name, handle, portfolio, or any identifying detail.

3. **No portfolio integration.** The system will not request, accept, or
   infer any user's holdings, balances, positions, or financial profile.

4. **No direct messaging engagement on trading advice.** If a user asks
   the bot for trading advice, the bot will not respond with
   recommendations. Any automated response should direct the user to the
   disclaimer and to independent professional advice.

5. **No paid or gated tier implying service.** The system remains
   non-commercial. Any future monetization requires full re-assessment of
   MiCA applicability, and this policy is void in that scenario until
   replaced.

6. **No "personalized alerts" feature.** Any future feature that tailors
   output to an individual user's interests, holdings, or objectives will
   be treated as a scope change and blocked until reviewed.

## What VolatiAI may continue to do

- Publish general signal digests to public channels
- Aggregate data from public sources
- Categorize signals by topic (crypto, health, weather, etc.)
- Provide clearly-labelled general information with disclaimers
- Link to third-party data sources and their terms

## Enforcement

The operator reviews this policy whenever:

- A new platform or channel is added
- A new feature is contemplated
- A new category of data source is added
- Any user interaction feature is proposed

Any violation of the commitments above is treated as a compliance incident
and must be documented and remediated.

## Disclaimer carried on all output

Every automated post carries:

    ⚠️  Informational only. Not financial, medical, or legal advice.
    🤖  Automated bot · AI-generated content · Non-commercial · Stateless

This is not decorative. It is evidence of the operator's intent to inform,
not to advise, and is aligned with FIN-FSA's expectation that crypto
marketing be "fair, clear, and not misleading."

## Limitations

This policy reflects the operator's reading of MiCA and FIN-FSA guidance as
of the policy date. It is not legal advice. A qualified legal professional
should be consulted before any change that affects this policy.
