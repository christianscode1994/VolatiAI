VolatiAI Privacy Note

VolatiAI does not collect, store, or process personal data.

The software operates locally and does not transmit user information to any
server. Activation keys (VAI‑LIC) are used only to verify that the software was
legitimately obtained. They are not linked to names, emails, or identifiable
information.

No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or logging of user behavior is performed.

If you purchased VolatiAI through a third-party platform (Gumroad, Paddle,
Lemon Squeezy, Stripe), any personal data you provided during purchase is
handled solely by that platform under their own privacy policies.
