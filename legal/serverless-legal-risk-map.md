# Serverless Legal Risk Map
### Understanding VolatiAI’s Legal Exposure

This document maps potential legal risk areas and shows how VolatiAI avoids them through its serverless, stateless architecture.

---

## 1. Infrastructure Risk
### Potential Risk:
Operating servers may trigger:
- NIS2 obligations  
- Hosting compliance  
- Security audits  

### VolatiAI Mitigation:
- 100% serverless  
- GitHub Actions ephemeral runners  
- No infrastructure ownership  

Risk Level: **None**

---

## 2. Data Risk
### Potential Risk:
Storing personal data triggers GDPR.

### VolatiAI Mitigation:
- No personal data  
- No accounts  
- No cookies  
- No logs  
- No identifiers  

Risk Level: **None**

---

## 3. Financial Risk
### Potential Risk:
Handling payments triggers AML/KYC.

### VolatiAI Mitigation:
- No payments  
- No subscriptions  
- No fiat  
- No crypto custody  

Risk Level: **None**

---

## 4. Content Risk
### Potential Risk:
Processing copyrighted or private content.

### VolatiAI Mitigation:
- Only public data sources  
- No user uploads  
- No private messages  

Risk Level: **None**

---

## 5. Operational Risk
### Potential Risk:
Persistent state or databases create compliance overhead.

### VolatiAI Mitigation:
- No databases  
- No persistent storage  
- All state is ephemeral  

Risk Level: **None**

---

## 6. Summary
VolatiAI’s serverless architecture eliminates:
- Infrastructure risk  
- Data risk  
- Financial risk  
- Content risk  
- Operational risk  

VolatiAI is legally minimal and compliance‑safe.
