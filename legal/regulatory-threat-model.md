# Regulatory Threat Model
### Forward-Looking Analysis for VolatiAI

This document outlines potential future regulatory threats and explains how VolatiAI’s architecture remains safe.

---

## 1. GDPR Expansion
### Threat:
GDPR may expand to include new categories of data.

### VolatiAI Defense:
- No personal data  
- No user tracking  
- No identifiers  
- No accounts  

Even expanded GDPR would not apply.

---

## 2. AI Regulation (EU AI Act)
### Threat:
AI systems may face classification requirements.

### VolatiAI Defense:
- VolatiAI is not a biometric system  
- Not a recommender system  
- Not a profiling system  
- Not a decision-making system  
- Not a high-risk AI category  

VolatiAI is a **public-data analytics engine**, not a regulated AI system.

---

## 3. Financial Regulation (MiCA, AML/KYC)
### Threat:
Crypto analytics tools may be scrutinized.

### VolatiAI Defense:
- No custody  
- No trading  
- No exchange operations  
- No user accounts  
- No financial services  

VolatiAI is not a financial entity.

---

## 4. Infrastructure Regulation (NIS2)
### Threat:
Operators of essential services may face obligations.

### VolatiAI Defense:
- No servers  
- No networks  
- No infrastructure  
- No hosting  

VolatiAI uses GitHub’s infrastructure only.

---

## 5. Data Localization Laws
### Threat:
Countries may require local storage.

### VolatiAI Defense:
- No storage  
- No databases  
- No user data  

Localization laws do not apply.

---

## 6. Summary
VolatiAI is resilient against future regulation because it:
- Stores nothing  
- Tracks nothing  
- Processes no personal data  
- Provides no financial services  
- Operates no infrastructure  

VolatiAI is future-proof by design.
